PLAYER PHOTOS
=============
Put a picture here named after the player (png, jpg, jpeg or webp).
It shows up automatically in the card (rectangle) AND in every circle
(search, trending, favourites, squad, compare). No code or database change needed.
Restart is NOT required - just refresh the page.

Tip: portrait photo, face in the upper half, about 800 x 1000 px (4:5).
